About the author

Gareth Andrew Mackenzie

Gareth Andrew Mackenzie is an author with a professional background in analytics, risk, internal controls, and process design. His work is grounded in systems thinking: understanding how inputs, controls, decisions, and feedback loops shape outcomes.

BUILT applies that same lens to wealth, ownership, leverage, resilience, and choice.
