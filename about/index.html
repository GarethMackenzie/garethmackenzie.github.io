About the author

Gareth Andrew Mackenzie

Gareth Andrew Mackenzie is an author with a professional background in analytics, risk, and process design. He applies systems thinking to wealth, decision-making, and the architecture behind durable outcomes.

His work asks a practical question: how do you deliberately construct a life with greater ownership, leverage, resilience, and choice?

Professional background

Where the systems thinking comes from.

Gareth's professional work is in data analytics, with a focus on financial services and insurance analytics, risk analytics and internal controls, and process optimisation. His analytical toolkit includes SQL, Python, and Power BI.

That background is the lens BUILT is written through: treating wealth not as a matter of motivation, but as a system with inputs, controls, and feedback loops, the same discipline applied to risk models and internal controls, turned toward personal financial decision-making.
