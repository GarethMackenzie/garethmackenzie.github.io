Published essays

Ten themes, explored in depth.

Long-form essays built from the core logic of BUILT.

01 · Capital allocation

Capital allocation is the operating system of wealth.

Income creates capacity. Allocation decides what that capacity becomes.

Read essay → 02 · Leverage

Leverage should multiply a system, not a weakness.

Amplification increases the output of a system and the consequences of a bad one.

Read essay → 03 · Asymmetric risk

Design the shape of the risk before you chase the upside.

Bound the downside, preserve optionality, and keep the ability to make the next decision.

Read essay → 04 · Business systems

A system turns good decisions into repeatable outcomes.

Triggers, standards, controls, feedback, and ownership turn experience into structure.

Read essay → 05 · Decision-making

Decision quality depends on the frame before the choice.

Better judgement begins with the question, constraints, evidence, and trade-offs.

Read essay → 06 · Compounding

Compounding rewards continuity more than intensity.

Useful gains become powerful when they survive long enough to influence the next cycle.

Read essay → 07 · Information advantage

Information advantage is about better decisions, not more data.

Useful information reduces uncertainty where uncertainty is expensive.

Read essay → 08 · Ownership

Ownership changes the relationship between effort and outcome.

Part of present effort can be converted into assets that continue to influence the future.

Read essay → 09 · Scale

Scale should expand what already works.

Durable scale begins with repeatability, capacity, controls, and clear constraints.

Read essay → 10 · Strategic execution

Strategy becomes real only when execution has a system.

Priorities, ownership, sequencing, feedback, and operating rhythm turn intent into progress.
