Gareth Andrew Mackenzie

Gareth Andrew Mackenzie is the author of BUILT: How Wealth Is Deliberately Constructed. His professional background spans analytics, risk, internal controls, and process design, and his work applies systems thinking to wealth and decision-making.

BUILT

BUILT examines the systems, decisions, and principles behind durable wealth, connecting capital, leverage, ownership, risk, information, scale, and compounding into one practical framework.
