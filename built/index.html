The book

BUILT
How Wealth Is Deliberately Constructed.

A practical framework for people who want to move beyond income and understand the architecture behind ownership, leverage, resilience, and long-term financial advantage.

BUILT examines wealth as a constructed system rather than a motivational outcome. It focuses on the decisions, structures, and principles that can make progress more durable over time.

The book connects capital, leverage, ownership, risk, execution, information, scale, and compounding into one framework instead of presenting them as isolated tactics.
