The premise

Earning more is not a complete strategy.

BUILT is for people who want to understand how lasting financial advantage is created on purpose.

It moves beyond motivation, hustle, and short-term tactics, and focuses on the economic structure you build around your life, the kind that gets stronger with time instead of depending on you every day.
