Is BUILT a get-rich-quick book?

No. BUILT examines the systems, allocation decisions, ownership and risk behind durable wealth. It offers a framework for thinking, not a promise of returns.

Do I need a finance background?

No. The book is written in English for readers who want to understand how decisions and systems shape outcomes.

Who will get the most from the book?

Entrepreneurs, professionals, executives and investors who want to connect effort, capital and expertise to durable outcomes.

About the author

The thinking behind the work.

Gareth Andrew Mackenzie is an author with a professional background in analytics, risk, internal controls, and process design.

His work applies systems thinking to wealth and decision-making: understanding how inputs, controls, allocation, feedback, and compounding shape outcomes.
