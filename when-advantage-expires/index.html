Introduction · Excerpt

When What Made You Valuable Becomes Easy

Imagine waking tomorrow to discover that the thing you spent ten years becoming good at has become easy.

Not worthless.

Easy.

Customers still want the outcome. Employers still need the work. Your skill still matters.

But thousands more people can now produce something acceptable. Technology makes delivery faster. Competitors gain access to capabilities that once required years to build.

You have not become less capable.

What changed is the scarcity surrounding your capability.

That distinction is the subject of this book.
